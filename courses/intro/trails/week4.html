Trail Briefing

Medicines can relieve symptoms and support recovery. Their availability does not eliminate risk: how a medicine is used, other substances, a person's health, and clinical supervision all matter.

This stop connects medication safety with treatment and referral decisions. Withdrawal management addresses immediate stabilization; ongoing care addresses the broader substance use disorder and the person's recovery goals.

By the End of This Lecture, You Should Be Able To:

  • Explain the roles of prescription and over-the-counter medications in substance misuse.
  • Identify basic principles of pharmacotherapy for treating substance use disorders.
  • Identify goals and practices of detox protocols.
  • Define key terms related to prescription drugs, OTC misuse, pharmacotherapy, detoxification, tolerance, and addiction.
