Trail Briefing

No single model completely explains every experience of addiction. This Trail Stop introduces four influential ways addiction has been understood in U.S. society: the moral view, disease model, public health perspective, and sociological model.

Each lens emphasizes something different. The lens we choose can shape the language we use, what we believe causes addiction, whether we emphasize punishment or treatment, and what kinds of supports we consider appropriate.

By the End of This Trail Stop, You Should Be Able To:

  • Describe the moral, disease, public health, and sociological models of addiction.
  • Recognize the assumptions and priorities emphasized by each model.
  • Compare how different models may shape treatment, policy, stigma, and helping responses.
  • Explain why a single model may not capture the full complexity of substance use and addiction.
  • Apply multiple models to a realistic client situation.
  • Use model awareness to support respectful, evidence-informed, person-centered practice.
