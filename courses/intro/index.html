Welcome to the Trailhead

Understanding substance use and addiction begins with more than memorizing drug names or definitions. It begins with learning how biology, psychology, families, communities, culture, policy, and human experience intersect.

Throughout the Foundations Trail, you will explore essential concepts in chemical dependency while practicing the kinds of observation, reflection, evidence evaluation, and decision-making skills used by helping professionals.

Each week opens another Trail Stop. Some stops may contain interactive challenges, videos, case situations, reflection points, evidence investigations, or downloadable achievements.

A Trail Note from Dr. Holdaway

Substance use and addiction are complex human experiences. As you move through this course, try to resist explanations that reduce people to a diagnosis, a substance, a behavior, or a single life experience.

Good helping professionals ask questions, consider context, evaluate evidence, recognize the limits of their knowledge, and remain willing to learn from the people and communities they serve.

You do not have to reach the summit all at once. Counselor development happens one thoughtful step at a time.
