Welcome to the Academy

Every helping professional begins somewhere. Here, your learning unfolds along a series of paths designed to help you develop knowledge, judgment, compassion, self-awareness, and practical counseling skills.

The journey will take you through the science of addiction, ethical decision-making, counseling practice, cultural humility, behavioral addictions, group facilitation, professional development, and the lived experiences of individuals, families, and communities.

You do not need to know everything before beginning. You simply need curiosity, a willingness to reflect, and the courage to keep learning.

Your Counselor Development Journey

The Academy is designed to help you move beyond simply knowing information and begin applying it in thoughtful, ethical, culturally responsive, and realistic ways.

Learn

Explore research, theory, terminology, treatment approaches, ethical standards, and evidence-informed practices.

Practice

Work through client cases, counseling choices, branching scenarios, interactive videos, reflection activities, and professional decisions.

Grow

Develop cultural humility, professional judgment, communication skills, self-awareness, and your identity as a helping professional.

A Note from Dr. Holdaway

The work of helping people affected by substance use and other addictive behaviors requires knowledge, empathy, ethical judgment, cultural humility, curiosity, and continued learning.

Throughout the Academy, you will be asked not only what you know, but how you might apply that knowledge when working with real people, families, groups, organizations, and communities.

There will not always be one simple answer. Human beings are complex, counseling requires judgment, and meaningful helping relationships require us to remain open to learning.

Take your time on the trail. Notice what challenges you. Consider the evidence. Reflect on your own perspective. And keep developing the counselor you want to become.
